HOPSCOTCH RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. STANDARD RETURNS (change of mind, wrong size, colour)
- Allowed within 30 days of delivery.
- Item must be unworn, unwashed, tags attached, original packaging.
- After 30 days, or if worn / washed / tags removed, a standard return is not accepted.

2. MANUFACTURING DEFECTS
- Covered for 90 days from delivery, even if worn or washed as per the care label.
- Examples: stitching coming apart, sole peeling in normal use, zip or button failing, colour bleed when washed as instructed, print cracking, elastic losing stretch, flaws present on arrival.
- Photos are required before replacement or refund.
- Defect claims are not limited by the 30-day standard return window.

3. NOT COVERED
- Misuse or accidents (pets, cuts, stains after use, rough use).
- Normal wear and tear.
- Damage from ignoring the care label.

4. MUST GO TO A HUMAN AGENT
Do not auto-decide these cases:
- Child safety (choking, loose small parts, injury).
- Skin reaction or allergy.
- Order value above INR 5,000.
- Unclear or conflicting facts, including defect vs misuse.
